## Tape > Tokenomics > Narrative for Short-Term Altcoin Trades

**Severity: HIGH — Starting with tokenomics and forcing the tape to fit will consistently lose money in trending altcoins.**

Adapted from stock pitfall #03 (Tape > DCF). For altcoin trades held < 30 days, the priority ordering is:
1. **Price action / tape** (what is the token doing right now)
2. **On-chain structure** (OI, L/S ratio, squeeze signals)
3. **Narrative** (what story is driving the token)
4. **Tokenomics** (supply schedule, FDV, unlock calendar)

**Why it matters**: Tokenomics is a **trailing framework**, not a leading indicator. A token can be "fundamentally overvalued" by any tokenomics metric (FDV/MC, circulating vs total supply) for weeks or months while a squeeze drives it 5–10x. The tokenomics will catch up eventually, but you will be liquidated on your short before "eventually" arrives. This is a direct application of the stock-world lesson: DCF is irrelevant for a 30-day trade window.

**How to apply**:
- Open every altcoin analysis by describing the tape first: last 3 daily candles, volume profile, EMA20 distance, OI trend
- Second layer: squeeze structure — OI/MC, L/S ratio, smart money
- Third layer: narrative — what catalyst or story is fueling the move
- Last layer: tokenomics — only relevant for position sizing and exit timeline, not for directional bias in a squeeze
- For trades > 60 days or spot accumulation strategies, tokenomics moves up in priority

**Crypto-specific note**: Unlike equities where "the market will eventually recognize fair value," in altcoins, tokens can go to zero OR to 100x purely on manipulation and narrative. Tokenomics has even less short-term predictive power than DCF does in stocks.
