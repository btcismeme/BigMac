## KOL and CT Consensus Is a Sample, Not a Signal — And They're Usually Positioned

**Severity: MEDIUM — Crypto Twitter (CT) KOLs are almost always positioned before they tweet. Their signal is about their own exit, not your entry.**

Adapted from stock pitfall #14 (channel check sample bias). In stocks, a single agency channel check is n=1. In crypto, a single KOL's bullish tweet is also n=1 — and the selection bias is worse, because KOLs tweet when they *want* price to move (when they hold a position and need retail buyers). The tweet IS the manipulation signal, not information independent of the manipulation.

**Why it matters**: If a KOL with 500K followers tweets "this token has insane fundamentals, setting up for a massive squeeze" and is themselves long, the tweet:
1. Attracts retail buyers (demand)
2. Pushes price higher
3. Allows the KOL to sell into retail demand
4. After KOL sells: price retraces, retail bags

This cycle is so common it has a name in CT: "pump and dump via KOL narrative." The KOL may not be malicious — they may genuinely be bullish — but their entry point is always earlier than your entry if you're reading their tweet.

**How to apply**:
- **Do not enter a position based on a single KOL tweet**: this is n=1, heavily self-selected
- Before taking a KOL signal, check:
  - Was the KOL bullish on this token **before** the pump started? (early = possibly legitimate edge)
  - Is this the first tweet, or have they been tweeting about it for weeks? (first tweet during the pump = likely exiting)
  - Are **3+ independent, non-correlated** voices saying the same thing? (real signal threshold)
- **Treat peak KOL/CT excitement as a fade signal**, not a continuation:
  - When every CT account is talking about a token: the information is priced in, the exit is near
  - When nobody is talking about it: that is when the setup forms
- **Check if KOL has a referral code or affiliate deal** with the exchange/project: financial incentive to shill
- **Useful KOLs for altcoin squeeze**: those who provide raw on-chain data (OI/MC, L/S ratios, funding rates) rather than price targets or narratives — data over opinion

**Cross-reference**: Use KOL information as a third or fourth layer, after tape (OI/MC, L/S ratio, EMA20) and smart money signals.
