## Squeeze Altcoins Are Manipulator-Tape by Definition

**Severity: HIGH — A squeeze token is, by construction, being orchestrated by large players. Align with the manipulation, don't fight it.**

Adapted from stock pitfall #12. A squeeze altcoin is not a "market" — it is a coordinated pump by whales who are long against a large retail short base. The manipulation is legal in crypto (unlike stocks) and explicit. Your edge is **knowing the mechanism and riding it**, not predicting organic price movement.

**Why it matters**: In stocks, manipulator-tape names (like MSTR, COIN, PLTR) mix real fundamental movements with occasional algorithmic manipulation. In squeeze altcoins, the *entire move* is the manipulation. The whales pump, retail shorts get liquidated, whales sell into the cascade. Understanding this changes how you trade: you are not "investing" or "analyzing fundamentals" — you are timing a predetermined mechanical event.

**How to apply for squeeze altcoins**:
- **Long the squeeze, not the project**: your thesis is mechanical (OI/MC, L/S ratio), not fundamental
- **Don't add on fundamentals**: a positive news article or KOL tweet during a squeeze is likely a signal that whales are near their exit — they need narrative to attract buyers for them to sell into
- **Scale out into pumps, not into dips** (opposite of DCA): in a manipulator tape, "buying the dip" on the way down can be catching a falling knife after the pump is over
- **Exit before the narrative peak**: the most bullish tweets, CT threads, and KOL endorsements tend to appear at the pump top — treat peak narrative as an exit signal, not a continuation signal
- **No shorting the manipulator** (see pitfall #05): even when the pump looks "done," the whale can extend it arbitrarily

**Altcoin vs stock manipulator comparison**:
| Stock (MSTR/APP/PLTR) | Squeeze Altcoin |
|-----------------------|-----------------|
| Mix of real flows + manipulation | Primarily orchestrated |
| Options premium-selling edge | Perp long + exit into pump |
| IV-based structure | OI/L/S ratio-based structure |
| Jade Lizard / Iron Condor | Scale-out long + trail stop |
