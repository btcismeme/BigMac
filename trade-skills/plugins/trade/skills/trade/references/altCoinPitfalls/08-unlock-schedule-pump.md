## Approaching Token Unlock Dates Create Pump Incentive

**Severity: MEDIUM — Token unlock events give insiders and the team strong incentive to pump price before the unlock, not after.**

When a token has a large vesting unlock approaching (team tokens, VC allocations, ecosystem rewards), the parties who will receive those tokens have a **financial incentive to maximize the price at the unlock date**. This creates a structural bias toward upward price manipulation in the weeks leading up to the unlock. The closer the unlock, the stronger the incentive. After the unlock, these parties often dump — so the dynamic flips.

**Why it matters**: This is the crypto equivalent of a company doing earnings management before an insider lockup expiry. The team can't control earnings, but they can influence price through market communication, exchange listings, partnerships, and direct trading. The unlock date is a hard deadline, so the incentive is time-bounded and measurable.

**How to apply**:
- For any altcoin squeeze candidate, check the **unlock schedule** as part of the initial setup:
  - CoinGlass → token → "Unlock Schedule" or "Vesting" tab
  - TokenUnlocks.app (alternative source)
- Thresholds for upgrade in conviction:
  - Unlock in > 90 days: minor factor
  - Unlock in 30–90 days: meaningful — mention in thesis
  - **Unlock in < 30 days**: significant pump incentive — upgrade squeeze conviction
  - Unlock in < 7 days: very high risk of short-term pump (then dump after unlock)
- Check the **size** of the unlock relative to circulating supply:
  - < 5% of circulating supply: minor
  - 5–20%: notable
  - **> 20%**: major — both the pump incentive and the post-unlock dump risk are elevated
- Cross-reference with OI/MC (pitfall #01) — high OI/MC + imminent large unlock = very high squeeze probability
- **Exit timing**: plan to exit long position **before** the actual unlock date, not after. Dump risk is highest in the 24–48H after tokens are released.

**Data source**: [CoinGlass](https://www.coinglass.com) → token → Unlock tab. Also visible in the market cap panel showing fully diluted valuation (FDV) vs current MC — large FDV/MC gap implies significant future unlock supply.
