## When the Squeeze Premise Breaks, FLIP to Flat — Don't Hold

**Severity: HIGH — Holding through squeeze invalidation converts a profitable long into a losing bag.**

Adapted from stock pitfall #04. If the structural conditions that justified the squeeze long stop being true, exit immediately. The squeeze thesis is not a "fundamental investment" — it is a structural setup with specific, measurable preconditions. When those preconditions break, the setup is dead.

**Why it matters**: Squeeze longs are held because of mechanics, not because of belief in the project. When the mechanics change (shorts covered, whales exiting, OI collapsing), the reason to be long is gone. Holding because "I believe in the token" or "I'm already up 50% why exit" is sunk-cost bias in a context where the remaining upside is now near zero and downside is significant.

**How to apply**:
- **Write down the squeeze preconditions at entry**:
  - OI/MC > X
  - 多空比人数 < Y
  - Smart money long > $Z
  - Price above 4H EMA20
- **Review after every major session**:
  - OI/MC dropping sharply → shorts covering, squeeze may be ending → reduce
  - 多空比人数 rising toward 1.0 → retail shorts closing → fuel exhausted → reduce
  - Smart money long position dropping → whales exiting → EXIT
  - Daily close below EMA20 → structure broken → EXIT
- Exiting a long is not losing — it is discipline
- Going from long → flat is the correct response to invalidation
- Going from long → short is **only** valid when ALL squeeze signals have reversed AND you have identified a structural short thesis (very rare — usually just go flat)

**Key difference from stocks**: In stocks, you might give a position 1–2 weeks to reassert after apparent invalidation. In altcoins, squeeze unwinds can be sudden and violent (−30% in hours). Exits must be faster.
