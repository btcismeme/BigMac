## 4H and Daily EMA20 is the Key Structural Level for Squeeze Coins

**Severity: MEDIUM — EMA20 is the one TA tool that retains validity in squeeze environments.**

Even though most technical analysis is invalid during a squeeze (pitfall #06), the **4-hour and daily EMA20** serves as a structural health indicator. Squeeze tokens in an active squeeze phase will almost always find support at the 4H EMA20 during pullbacks (洗盘), even when those pullbacks are violent (-15% to -30% wicks). As long as the 4H and daily candles continue to close **above EMA20**, the squeeze structure remains intact.

**Why it matters**: The EMA20 (20-period exponential moving average) on higher timeframes reflects where large holders are positioned on average. In a squeeze, the whales who are long (the 庄家) will defend this level with purchases because it represents their average cost basis and acts as the line between "squeeze intact" and "squeeze failed." Losing EMA20 on the daily typically means the squeeze is exhausted or the whales are exiting.

**How to apply**:
- **For active long positions**: check 4H EMA20 daily
  - Price pulling back to 4H EMA20 and bouncing → normal, hold position
  - Violent wick through 4H EMA20 but 4H candle **closes above** → still intact, potential buy-the-dip
  - 4H candle **closes below** EMA20 → yellow flag, reduce position
  - **Daily candle closes below EMA20** → red flag, exit most or all of long position
- **For entry**: a clean 4H bounce off EMA20 with rising OI and improving L/S ratio is a high-quality entry signal
- **For exit signal**: 2 consecutive daily closes below EMA20 is a strong signal to exit and reassess
- Do NOT use EMA50 or EMA100 as the primary reference — EMA20 is the correct one for squeeze dynamics

**Note**: During the most violent pump phases of a squeeze, price can be 40–80% above EMA20. This is normal. The EMA20 is not a reversal signal when price is above it — it is an anchor.
