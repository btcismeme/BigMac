## Take-Profit Discipline is More Important in Altcoins Than in Stocks

**Severity: HIGH — Altcoin pumps reverse faster and more violently than stock moves. Book profits early and often.**

Adapted from stock pitfall #13. In altcoins, the pump-dump cycle is compressed: what takes a stock 3 weeks to do, an altcoin can do in 3 hours, and the reversal is equally fast. Waiting for "full target" in a squeeze token almost always results in giving back 30–60% of the gain. Book profits at defined intervals, trail the rest.

**Why it matters**: In a squeeze, the final 30% of the move happens in the last 5% of the time. By the time the "full target" is obvious to everyone, it is also obvious to the whales who are now selling into your hands. The correct approach is to be **earlier than feels comfortable** on exits — the market will take care of the confirmation that you sold too soon.

**How to apply**:
- **Scale-out framework for squeeze longs**:
  - At +50% gain: exit 33% of position (lock in, never give back)
  - At +100% gain: exit another 33% (position is now entirely house money)
  - Remaining 33%: trail with a stop at the most recent 4H EMA20 touch — let it run until structure breaks
- **Hard rules**:
  - Never give back more than 50% of peak unrealized gain from a single position
  - If smart money long drops > 20% from peak: exit all remaining
  - If 4H daily closes below EMA20 twice in a row: exit all remaining
- **For the largest positions (> $50K)**: accelerate exit — begin scaling at +30% because the position itself moves the market
- **Altcoin-specific**: liquidity dries up faster than stocks. Large positions need to scale out over multiple 4H candles, not in a single market order

**Resist the narrative**: "It's going to $X, I should hold." In a squeeze, the narrative is always most bullish at the top. Take profit mechanically, not narratively.
