## DRAM vs NAND Deep Dive — The Structural Difference

This is the key framework for deciding whether to treat MU as SNDK proxy or as a differentiated name.

| Dimension | NAND (SNDK) | DRAM (MU-dominant) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Supply concentration** | Samsung, Kioxia/SNDK, SK Hynix, Micron, Western Digital = 5 major players | Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron = 3 players (oligopoly) | DRAM better pricing discipline |
| **AI demand linkage** | Storage (SSDs) for data; less tied to inference/training speed | HBM = directly stacked on GPU; server DRAM = training pools | DRAM more strategic for AI |
| **Cycle volatility** | Historically ±40–60% price swings | Historically ±25–40% price swings | DRAM less volatile |
| **Capex cycle** | Long; oversupply cycles common | Samsung disciplined post-2023 losses | DRAM supply more controlled |
| **Q3 2026 pricing** | Softening (SNDK guide) | Stable to slightly rising (DRAM spot data) | DRAM holding better |
| **HBM** | No HBM product | MU is HBM3E certified by NVDA | MU-only moat |

**Bottom line**: DRAM and NAND are related but not the same commodity. SNDK's soft NAND guide is NOT a read-through for MU's DRAM business. However, MU's NAND segment (30–35% of revenue) could face similar pressure.

## Pitfall Mapping

### Pitfall #9 — Preconditions ≠ Direction
SNDK's soft NAND guide is a **precondition** for concern about MU. But it does NOT determine MU's direction. MU's DRAM segment operates on different fundamentals. Do not map SNDK's guide directly onto MU's Q4 guide without checking DRAM-specific data.

### Pitfall #1 — Consensus Not Bearish (or Bullish Enough)
82% of MU analysts are Buy-rated. Consensus is bullish. This is NOT a contrarian buy signal — it means the consensus is already positioned. **The edge is in the divergence**: those (like Citi Aug 7) who are cutting vs the JPMorgan/BofA bull camp. Watch for PT revision direction.

### Pitfall #20 — Post-Earnings Momentum vs Fade
MU is pre-ER. The temptation is to treat SNDK's post-ER move as a read-through for MU's upcoming ER. RESIST. MU has different product mix. The setup for MU's ER is independent — especially if DRAM pricing holds steady or NVDA confirms HBM demand in late August.

### Pitfall #3 — Tape Over DCF
NVDA +8.38% in 5 days while SNDK -5.89% — the tape is ALREADY telling you the market prefers AI-compute memory (HBM/DRAM) over storage NAND. Follow the tape: MU at +5.80% is moving with NVDA, not with SNDK.
