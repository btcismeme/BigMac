## Lessons & Framework: Pre-IPO Sector Proxy Rotation Plays

### Step-by-Step Framework for Similar Setups

**1. Identify Mega-IPO Timeline** (6-12 months forward):

- Source: Company guidance, founder interviews, banker chatter, SEC regulatory timeline
- Confidence level: 70%+ before accumulation entry

**2. Map Sector Supply Chain**:

- List 5-10 direct suppliers / adjacent companies with revenue exposure
- Filter for: (a) publicly traded, (b) >30% beta to mega-IPO company, (c) illiquid (wide spreads = entry opportunity)
- RDW ranked #1 on all three criteria

**3. Accumulation Strategy (Not Mean Reversion)**:

- Pre-IPO sector rotations reward **volume accumulators**, not mean-reversion timing.
- Entry rule: Accumulate 1-2% portfolio dips every $0.50-1.00 decline over 6-month window.
- Avoid single-day reversal entries (too noisy); wait for 3+ day consolidation.

**4. Hedge Redeemable / Merger Arb Risk**:

- If position involves SPAC residual (RDW's $10 redemption strike), short synthetic calls 30-40% above merger arb ceiling.
- Naked short calls are hedged by long equity, reducing tail risk.

**5. Structure Choice (NOT Spreads)**:

- Sector rotation plays have undefined upside (dependent on IPO demand momentum, not earnings).
- Avoid credit spreads / iron condors (cap upside exactly when you expect unlimited rally).
- Use: (a) equity + leverage, (b) LEAPS calls (long vega benefit if IV crushes _after_ IPO pop).
- Short calls only for premium harvesting, not directional bet.

**6. Exit Rules**:

- **Profit-taking**: At IPO announcement, trim 30% of position (lock in pre-IPO pop).
- **Stop-loss**: If mega-IPO timeline slips >6 months beyond guidance, liquidate (thesis broken).
- **Tail-risk exit**: If sector IV drops >40% (from 78% to <50%) on no news, sector momentum is exhausted — exit.
