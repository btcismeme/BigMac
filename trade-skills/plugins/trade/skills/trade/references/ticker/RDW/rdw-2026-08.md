## Lessons & Framework: Post-IPO Proxy Management

### Step-by-Step Framework for Post-IPO Proxy Rotation Management

**1. Pre-IPO Exit Discipline** (validated in May→June):

- Execute planned 30% trim at IPO roadshow announcement (not IPO pricing — roadshow = maximum pre-IPO hype).
- Do NOT wait for IPO first-day pop in the proxy — capital rotates from proxy INTO the IPO on pricing night.
- Lesson confirmed: RDW peaked June 4 (roadshow day), not June 12 (IPO day).

**2. Post-IPO Flush as Entry Window**:

- Expect 15-25% flush from pre-IPO peak as proxy holders exit into IPO.
- Flush duration: 2-5 trading days (rotation is mechanical, not fundamental — fast).
- Entry signal: Volume spike + RSI approaching 35-40 (not fully oversold, but washed).
- RDW hit $12.88 (26% below $17.50 peak) in 9 calendar days — within model range.

**3. Thesis Upgrade Check After Flush**:

- After flush: re-assess whether the original catalyst has upgraded, maintained, or expired.
  - **Expired**: Proxy play only; no new catalyst. → Exit remaining position, look for next setup.
  - **Maintained**: Company fundamentals unchanged; market overreacted. → Re-accumulate for mean reversion.
  - **Upgraded**: New catalyst emerged (e.g., direct partnership, contract win). → New thesis, larger position.
- RDW in June = **Upgraded** (S-1 partnership language = new catalyst, not just continuation of proxy logic).

**4. Structure Shift Post-IPO**:

- Pre-IPO proxy: Equity + LEAPS + leverage (undefined upside; sector rotation has no cap).
- Post-IPO direct play: Equity + call spreads + downside puts (bounded catalyst; contract announcement is finite event).
- Never use pre-IPO options structures in post-IPO regime — the IV dynamics invert after the macro event.

**5. New Benchmark Discipline**:

- Pre-IPO: Proxy company's benchmark is the sector ETF (ARKX, ITA).
- Post-IPO: The IPO company (SPCE) becomes the primary benchmark for capital flow direction.
- SPCE-RDW divergence is now the most important daily signal — supersedes ARKX comparison.

**6. Exit Rules (Post-IPO Regime)**:

- **Profit-taking**: At direct contract announcement, trim 40% (lock in catalyst pop; rest runs with earnings).
- **Stop-loss**: $12.50 floor (below June 13 flush low; if broken, capital leaving space entirely).
- **Time stop**: If no direct contract news by end of Q3 2026, trim 25% (timeline slipping = partial de-risk).
- **Earnings exit**: If Q2 call doesn't mention SpaceX relationship, trim 30% (fundamental thesis weakening).
