## Risk Register

### 1. VIE Structure / China ADR Risk
- BZUN is a Cayman Islands holding company with VIE (Variable Interest Entity) contracts to access China operations
- US regulators have threatened to delist Chinese ADRs that fail PCAOB audits
- Risk: Company could be forced to delist from NASDAQ with little notice
- **Mitigation**: HKEX dual-listing (9991) provides partial protection; company has cooperated with audit requirements

### 2. GAAP vs Non-GAAP Profitability Gap
- GAAP net income still negative (-$27.04M TTM) due to FX losses, investment write-downs
- Q1 2026 non-GAAP EPS was barely positive (¥0.02)
- Bear case: Non-GAAP strip-outs may be masking real cash consumption
- **Counter**: FCF is genuinely positive ($46.52M TTM) — cash from operations = real

### 3. Altman Z-Score = 1.48
- Below 3.0 = elevated bankruptcy concern
- However: FCF positive, net cash $140.53M, operating income turning positive
- **Assessment**: Z-Score is backward-looking; current FCF trajectory contradicts bankruptcy risk

### 4. China Macro / Consumer Spending
- China consumer sentiment tied to property sector health, employment, geopolitics
- Any escalation in US-China tariffs (already present) could reduce brand spending in China
- **Signal to watch**: Q2 GMV data, BEC client count growth or churn

### 5. Currency Risk (CNY/USD)
- BZUN earns in CNY, trades on NASDAQ in USD
- CNY depreciation relative to USD = automatic P&L headwind when converted
- CNY at ~7.2 CNY/USD as of Aug 2026; any major move affects reported USD numbers

### 6. Competition from Alibaba/JD/PDD Ecosystem
- E-commerce platform giants are increasingly offering managed services in-house
- Risk: Brands may reduce reliance on third-party service providers like BEC
- **Counter**: Baozun's BBM pivot to brand ownership (vs. service fees) reduces this dependency
