## Framework 1: OI + Funding Rate (Positioning Analysis)

> **This is the most important dimension unique to BTC/crypto analysis.** Open Interest tells you HOW MUCH leverage is in the market; Funding Rate tells you WHICH SIDE is crowded.

### OI Quadrant Matrix

| Open Interest | Price Direction | Signal | Trade Implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Rising** | **Rising** | 🟢 Bullish conviction — longs adding | Trend continuation likely; ride momentum |
| **Rising** | **Falling** | 🔴 Short buildup — bears adding | Potential cascade lower; watch for squeeze trigger |
| **Falling** | **Rising** | 🟡 Short squeeze — shorts capitulating | Move may be fast but lacks follow-through; watch for fade |
| **Falling** | **Falling** | 🔴 Long liquidation / deleveraging | Capitulation event; may signal approaching bottom |

### Funding Rate Interpretation

| Funding Rate (8h) | Signal | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| > +0.10% | 🔴 Overheated longs — crowded long trade | Consider reducing; high risk of liquidation cascade |
| +0.05% to +0.10% | 🟡 Moderately bullish, some heat | Normal bull market; monitor OI for overextension |
| 0 to +0.05% | 🟢 Neutral to healthy | Best risk/reward for longs; neither side overloaded |
| 0 to -0.05% | 🟡 Moderate short bias | Potential squeeze setup if price trigger occurs |
| < -0.05% | 🟢 Crowded shorts — squeeze fuel | Strong upside potential; shorts paying longs = pressure to cover |

## Pitfall Mapping (Crypto-Specific)

- **Pitfall #1** (consensus not bearish): 82% of CoinGecko community is bullish — this is NOT a contrarian buy signal. Real bottom happens when KOLs are silent or capitulating, not when 82% are bullish.
- **Pitfall #3** (tape > opinion): BTC tape is the 24h chart and OI. Narratives about S2F, halving cycle, ETF floor are opinions. Price below key levels OVERRIDES any narrative.
- **Pitfall #9** (preconditions ≠ direction): ETF inflows + regulatory progress + strong on-chain = preconditions for recovery. They are NOT direction. Price can stay lower for longer while macro deteriorates.
- **Pitfall #16** (BSM drift vs vol): BTC has extreme vol regime (30-80% annualized vol). Options strategies must account for this. Selling premium in BTC requires wider strikes than equities.
- **Crypto-specific #A**: Positive funding rate + rising OI + bullish KOL consensus at same time = classic overleveraged setup → liquidation cascade risk is HIGH. Do NOT chase.
- **Crypto-specific #B**: ETF inflows are SPOT buying; OI rising is LEVERAGE. Only rising ETF flows + STABLE or FALLING OI = truly healthy bullish signal. Rising OI without ETF support = fragile.
